# Kestrel pricing (ver 1, proposals)

The software is free (MIT). You pay for the setup: we install the checks on your machine, map your exports, set your rules and thresholds, and walk your team through the first run. Prices in [brackets] are proposals for Francisco to set.

## Billing companies and MSOs
Partner rollout for up to 10 client practices: [$12,000], then [$2,000] a month.

## What is and is not included
Included: installation on your machine, column mapping, settings, one training session, the first report. Not included: billing, coding, legal, tax or clinical advice; submitting, filing or sending anything; logging in to your EHR, clearinghouse, a payer portal or a state system. Patient files stay on your machine. We sign a business associate agreement before we ever hold a file.
