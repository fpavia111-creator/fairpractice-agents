# Launch post (ver 1, draft; not posted)

**Small practices lose money and get fined for things a checklist would catch.**

A therapist bills a fourth unit the 8-minute rule does not allow. A hospice files an election notice a day late and loses the days for good. A home care visit gets billed with no matching EVV record and the state takes it back. An aide was added to the OIG exclusion list last month and nobody screened. A records request sits past 30 days.

Fairpractice is 30 small checks, one for each of those jobs. Each reads a file your systems already export and hands your team a worklist: what is wrong, how much it is worth, and what to do next. It runs on your own computer. Patient and staff names are replaced with pseudonyms before anything reaches the AI. It never bills, files, pays or sends anything; your people do. It does not read clinical notes or suggest codes.

The code is open source. We sell the setup: a fixed price per package, done with you over a shared screen.
