# Fixtures

Fictional data only: Bluewater Regional (a made-up hospital) and made-up physicians. Any likeness to real people is chance.

- `agreements.csv` and `physician-payments-2026.csv`: Planted: a medical director whose fourth payment goes over the 24,000 yearly cap, is paid 300 an hour against the 275 FMV high, and has no time log (Shore); on-call pay after the agreement ended in February (Tull); a consulting agreement never signed (Vale); a payment made before the agreement was signed (Arce); a physician paid with no agreement at all (Marsh); and two agreements ending within 60 days (Oyelaran, and Shore at year end).
- `settings.json`: example FMV hourly ranges by arrangement type; real ranges come from your valuation firm. `settings-typo.json` misspells a setting and is refused.
