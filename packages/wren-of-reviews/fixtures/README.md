# Fixtures

Fictional data only: Bluewater Family Medicine (a made-up practice) and made-up reviewers. Any likeness to real people is chance.

- `reviews.csv`: Planted: a safe reply to a 5-star review (G-101); a reply that uses the reviewer's name, a weekday and "your visit", "our records show" and "your lab" (G-102); a reply that says "your bill" (G-103); a 1-star review with no reply (G-104); a reply that repeats the reviewer's own health detail (Y-105); and a safe reply (G-106).
- `settings.json`: the practice name and office phone that safe replies use. `settings-typo.json` misspells a setting and is refused.
